# Included benchmark data

The support-routing examples in `overmind/data.py` are a project-authored synthetic benchmark created during implementation. They are dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**.

CC0 legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The laboratory makes no external provider calls to create this benchmark and does not ingest private conversations, scraped materials, or third-party datasets. The CC0 dedication applies to the project contribution and does not override any applicable upstream service terms. User-imported datasets retain their own licenses and require an explicit permission or license declaration.
